# Cibanna — Demo Builds

**Scan any dish. Cook any culture.** 🌍

Download the latest Android APK from the [Releases page](https://github.com/Ipaja80/cibanna-demo/releases).

## Install on Android
1. Download the `.apk` on your phone
2. Open it, allow "install from unknown sources"
3. Create your own account — the app is yours, under your name

## The three trims (all free to try in the demo)
- **Basic** — dish & barcode scanning, calories, restaurants & reservations, smart scales, voice ("Hi Cibanna")
- **Pro** — full nutrition, world cooking guides, cultural history (turn on 2FA to unlock)
- **Chef** — Chef Jay AI cooking masterclasses: the story of each dish, healthy-eating education, and step-by-step cooking (turn on 2FA to unlock)

## iPhone / any device
Open the website in your browser — full app, no install.
